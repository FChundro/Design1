How to use the prompts

  1. Copy the prompt into your AI assistant.
  2. Replace everything in [BRACKETS] with your own details. The more specific you are, the better the result.
  3. Follow up. The first answer is a draft. Reply with "make it shorter", "more casual", "give me 10 more" or "rewrite for a 19-year-old beginner".
  4. Add your voice. Edit the output so it sounds like you. People buy from people.
Pro move: set up your "brand brief" once. Paste this at the start of every new chat so the AI always knows who you are:
You are my content and business assistant. About me: I run the Instagram account [@HANDLE] about [NICHE]. My audience is [WHO THEY ARE, AGE, SITUATION]. They want [DESIRED RESULT] and struggle with [MAIN PROBLEM]. My tone is [e.g. direct, hype, friendly, no fluff]. I sell [PRODUCT/OFFER] for [PRICE]. Keep answers practical and skimmable.

01Find your product

Don't guess. Sell a solution to a problem your audience already talks about.

1. Profitable idea generatorGive me 20 digital product ideas for an audience of [AUDIENCE] interested in [NICHE]. For each: product type (ebook, template, course, prompt pack, community), the painful problem it solves, a suggested price, and how hard it is to create (1-5).
2. Pain point minerList the 25 most common frustrations, questions and fears that [AUDIENCE] have about [TOPIC]. Write each one in the exact words they'd type in a comment or Google search.
3. Comment analyzerHere are comments and DMs from my followers: [PASTE 20-50 COMMENTS]. Group them into themes, rank the themes by how often they appear, and suggest one paid product for the top 3 themes.
4. Idea scorecardScore these product ideas from 1-10 on: demand, urgency, how fast I can create it, how well it fits my audience, and upsell potential. Ideas: [LIST]. Show a table and recommend one.
5. Competitor gap finderPopular products in my niche are: [LIST PRODUCTS/PRICES]. What do buyers probably complain about with these? Suggest 5 angles where I could make something better, simpler or cheaper.
6. The irresistible promiseTurn this product idea into a one-sentence promise using the formula "Get [RESULT] in [TIME] without [PAIN]". Idea: [IDEA]. Give me 10 versions.
7. Validation pollWrite 5 Instagram Story poll and quiz sequences (3 slides each) to test whether my followers would buy [PRODUCT IDEA] before I build it.
8. Pre-sale postWrite an Instagram caption announcing that I'm creating [PRODUCT] and that the first [NUMBER] people who comment [KEYWORD] get it at [DISCOUNT]. Create urgency without sounding salesy.
9. Product name generatorGive me 30 catchy names for a [PRODUCT TYPE] that helps [AUDIENCE] [RESULT]. Mix styles: bold, playful, premium, and straightforward. Mark your top 5.
10. Product ladderDesign a value ladder for my brand: a free lead magnet, a $9-19 starter product, a $49-99 core product, and a $199+ premium offer, all around [NICHE]. Explain how each leads to the next.

★The 7-day launch plan

One focused week. About 1–2 hours a day.

Launch checklist

  • Product PDF/files exported and tested
  • Checkout works on mobile
  • Delivery email sends the download
  • Link in bio updated
  • "Start Here" highlight added
  • Comment keyword automation live
  • Launch Reel + carousel scheduled
  • Launch price end date announced
  • Thank-you + review request ready
  • Track clicks, sales and conversion
Realistic numbers: expect around 1–3% of people who see your offer to click and a few percent of those to buy. 50 sales at $19 = $950 from a single weekly launch. Then re-launch every 1–2 months with new bonuses.
