## Step 1: Create the account
1. Go to **https://gumroad.com** → **Start selling**.
2. Sign up with **virtualdmk@gmail.com** (your Digital Kings email).
3. Username: `digitalkings` (your store becomes `digitalkings.gumroad.com`). If taken, try `digitalkingio`.

## Step 2: Set up payouts (so the money reaches you)
1. **Settings → Payments**.
2. Add your payout method: **bank account** or **PayPal**. Gumroad lists which one it supports for your country.
3. Fill in the tax form Gumroad asks for.
- Gumroad takes a fee per sale (currently about **10% + payment processing**); check **Settings → Payments** for the exact rate.
- If your country isn't supported for payouts, use **Payhip** (payhip.com) or **Lemon Squeezy** (lemonsqueezy.com) instead. The same files and text work there.

## Collecting money from clients (services)
For services, like the Google-reviews system for local businesses, you have two options:
- **Gumroad:** create a second product, "Google Reviews Setup: monthly," as a **membership** (e.g. $150/month). Clients pay by card, and Gumroad bills them every month automatically.
- **PayPal or Stripe payment link:** create a payment link or invoice in your PayPal Business or Stripe dashboard and send it by WhatsApp or email.
Put everything in writing: what you deliver, the price, and how to cancel.
