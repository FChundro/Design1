## What's holding it back
1. **Empty profile.** No photo, bio or link, so the ~100 people who see each Reel have no reason to follow.
2. **Zero likes means the first 2 seconds aren't landing.** ~100 plays is Instagram's baseline test audience; nobody engaged, so it stopped pushing.
3. **Visible AI artifacts** (e.g. garbled text on a name badge) make the stories feel fake.
4. **Risky topics** ("scam", "hacking", "exploited", betting) can get reach limited and don't lead to a product you can sell.
5. **No call to action.** Captions don't ask for a comment, follow or save.
6. **Inactive for 5 months.** Consistency matters more than volume.
